Usage guide
Conditioning on player performance

Follow the patterns below when the condition depends on a player's performance. Use the player's ESPN display name.

  • say top 20 RB — a number and a pool
  • not an RB2 — ambiguous and easy to confuse
  • say top 2 RB — if you mean the second-best back, say the number
  • not the RB2 — ambiguous and easy to confuse
  • say 1,000 rushing yards, 1,500 yards from scrimmage, plays 14 games — all countable
  • not has a big year, stays healthy — nothing to measure
  • say top 20 flex, top 30 superflex — flex ranks RB/WR/TE together, superflex adds QBs; both work
Conditioning on manager performance

Follow the patterns below when the condition depends on a manager's season. Use the manager's last name.

  • say Topper makes the playoffs, Mansfield wins the championship, Gilmore gets a top 2 seed, Agne wins at least 9 games, Nelson finishes last

Every condition must be settleable with a number of some kind.

Player the condition depends on (optional)

How it gets measured

Scoring is the league's full PPR. Every condition is settled once the playoffs are over — the only thing to set here is which weeks of scoring the settlement reads.
