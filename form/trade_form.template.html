Conditions

The part that depends on what happens

Write it once, here. The boxes below only pin down which weeks it's measured over and which picks move each way.

Say it with a number

This gets settled in December against the actual scoring. Name the player, name the pool, give a number.

  • say top 20 RB — a number and a pool
  • not an RB2 — half the league reads that as top 12, half as top 24
  • say top 2 RB — if you mean the second-best back, say the number
  • not the RB2 — tier to some, rank to others; 22 spots apart
  • say 1,000 rushing yards, 1,500 yards from scrimmage, plays 14 games — all countable
  • not has a big year, stays healthy — nothing to measure
  • say top 20 flex, top 30 superflex — flex ranks RB/WR/TE together, superflex adds QBs; both work
Or hang it on a manager's season

Name the manager and say which finish you mean.

  • say Topper makes the playoffs, Maggie wins the championship, Topper gets a top 2 seed, she wins at least 9 games, he finishes dead last
  • also a surname is enough — unless two managers share one, then use a first name

A condition with no number in it can't settle itself — it lands on the commissioner in December, which is the argument you were trying to avoid. Agree on the number now.
